Back Up to the Cloud with Jianguoyun
---
Jianguoyun provides free cloud storage. You can back up your data to Jianguoyun for safe and convenient cloud backup, so even after changing devices or reinstalling the app, you can quickly restore all texts.

### Steps

Open the Jianguoyun website and register or sign in with the web version.

(If you open Jianguoyun on a phone, choose Log in to Web Version. If you do not have an account, create one first. After signing in, switch to the Me tab at the bottom and tap Request Desktop Site to enter the desktop web page.)

1. After signing in, click your username in the upper-right corner and choose Account Info.
2. Switch to the Security Options tab at the top.
3. Scroll to Third-party App Management at the bottom, click Add App, enter a name such as YesWriter, then click Generate Password.
4. According to the information shown on the page, go to YesWriter -> Backup & Restore -> Cloud Backup, select WebDAV, and fill in:
    * **Server address and account name: fill them in as shown on the web page.**
    * **Password: use the app password you just generated, which can be viewed in the Jianguoyun app list.**

After setup, you can sync texts to Jianguoyun. When changing devices or reinstalling the app, sign in to Jianguoyun to quickly restore all texts.
