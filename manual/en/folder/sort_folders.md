Sort Folders
---

Go to the Folders page, tap Sort By, and select the sorting method you need.

![](imgs/folder_sorting.png)

#### Manual Sorting

After selecting Manual Sorting, tap Manual Sorting again, then drag folders to adjust their order.

![](imgs/folder_sorting1.png)
