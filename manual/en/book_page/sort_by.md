Sort Texts and Categories
---
#### Steps

Tap Sort By and select the sorting method you need.

![](imgs/sort_by.png)

Manual Sorting
---
#### Steps

1. After selecting Manual Sorting, tap the Manual Sorting button beside it.

2. Choose text sorting or category sorting.

3. Long-press an item and drag it up or down to adjust the order.

![](imgs/manual_sorting.png)
