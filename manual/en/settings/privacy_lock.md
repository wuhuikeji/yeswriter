Use Privacy Lock
---
With Privacy Lock, you can lock specified texts, books, and folders.

#### Steps

1. Long-press the text, book, or folder you want to lock or unlock.

2. In the pop-up menu, tap Lock or Unlock to complete the action.

![](imgs/privacy_lock.png)

#### Tips

- In Me > Auto Lock & Privacy Lock, you can set fingerprint unlock or password unlock.

- Privacy Lock is a local temporary locking mechanism. After locking and backing up texts, if you restore them on another device, you can view them without unlocking again.
