Create Folder
---
#### Steps

1. Go to the Folders page.
2. Tap New Folder.
3. Enter the folder name.

![](imgs/new_folder.png)

#### Tips

1. Tap Custom Image to choose an image from your album as the folder cover.

2. Tap the colored round button on the right side of the folder name to set the name color.
