Create To-Do
---
#### Steps

1. Go to the To-Dos page.

2. Tap the + button in the lower-right corner.

3. Fill in the title, description, and other information.

4. Tap Confirm to create it.

![](imgs/new_to_do.png)

#### Tips

1. Continuous adding: on the New To-Do page, tap + in the lower-left corner to keep adding without leaving the page.

2. Item count: the bottom tab bar shows the number of currently unfinished items.

3. Pin item: tap Pin in the upper-right corner of an item in the list to move it to the top.
